# Intraday

1. Call `trading212(action="status")`. If the regular US session is closed, journal one short line and end the tick: no scan, no analyst, no order. Disconnected, disabled or uncertain means no order. Read history/reconcile uncertain submissions and notify the owner; never enable trading yourself. Read valuation, accounts, bot positions, history and journal. Native ledger and run context own capital, not generic scheduler defaults. Missing valuation blocks entries. At or below 2 total bot equity in EUR, no new exposure. No outside holdings, leverage, margin, short selling, borrowing, transfers or withdrawals. At your first tick of the New York trading day, journal the bot equity as the day's opening equity; if equity is 5% below that, make no new entries today and notify the owner (exits stay allowed).
2. Scan the market with `trading212(action="stocks", keyword="")`: the most traded US stocks that Trading 212 lists, with REFERENCE price, bid, ask, spread, 24h dollar volume, 24h range and the move since the open (reference prices in USD from Kraken xStocks public data, not Trading 212's own quote). Skip anything with a reference spread above 0.1% or under 50,000 in 24h dollar volume. Shortlist the 6 most promising new candidates by recent move, momentum, volume and range (single stocks with a 24h range of 1.5% or more; at most one broad ETF as a reference), rotate across ticks, and always include every stock the bot holds. For each shortlisted and held stock read `trading212(action="candles", symbol="<TICKER>", timeframe="15m", count="96")` and, if useful, the 1-hour candles. Then call
   `delegate(action="ask", agent="trading212_analyst", task="Assess evidence for intraday entries or exits; return actionable or no_trade", context="<native allocation in EUR, bot positions, shortlist with reference price/spread/24h dollar volume/range/move since open, minutes to close, compact candle summaries, journal and timestamps>")`.
   Wait for its answer. Never start helper loops or detached workers. An unavailable analyst, error, timeout or missing answer means no order; journal and notify. A no_trade report ends the tick quietly after journaling. Selling a held stock to fund a better one is allowed when the new expected gain beats the held stock's own outlook plus both legs' costs. Review every held stock for an exit every tick: target reached, invalidation broken or thesis stale.
3. Scorecard (a paper test): after the analyst's report each tick, log ONE call for the best candidate with no open call using `trading212(action="predict", symbol="<TICKER>", verdict="trade"|"pass", target_pct="...", stop_pct="...", horizon_hours="...", note="...")`. Use the analyst's target, invalidation and window; the horizon must end before the close. Read `action="scorecard"` first and never log numbers you do not expect. Logging places no order.
4. Only for an actionable proposal, take one preview with `trading212(action="preview", side="<buy|sell>", symbol="<TICKER>", amount="<exact decimal>")` and record the stock, quantity, limit price, EUR notional, `estimated_round_trip_pct` with its components and labels, the cost guard fields and the timestamp. It is evidence only. The round-trip cost comes from the preview, never from memory: the target must be at least 1.5 times that cost and at least 0.8%, with the stop no farther than two thirds of the target and the horizon ending before the close; otherwise no order, journal the near-miss. Propose a bounded intent naming side, exact pair, requested and maximum amount, maximum slippage percent, a cost bound at least 0.15 percentage points above the preview's round-trip cost, and a UTC validity deadline. Entries are roughly 15 to 25 percent of bot equity, and prefer entries of at least 8 EUR when equity allows, going above 25 percent of equity inside the 40% cap to get there. A buy whose preview has `cost_guard_blocks_buy` true would be refused by the native service: pass on it and do not propose it.
5. Call `delegate(action="ask", agent="trading212_risk_reviewer", task="Approve, reject or mark incomplete this bounded trade intent before the order", context="<market report; preview; the bounds; minutes to close; native status, cash, positions, equity, limits and valuation timestamp>")`.
   Analysis precedes review: consult sequentially and on demand. An unavailable reviewer, error, timeout, missing answer, reject or incomplete means NO ORDER: journal and notify. Require an explicit approve binding side, exact pair, requested amount, maximum amount, maximum slippage percent, permitted costs/spread, supported cost basis, UTC validity deadline and the allocation snapshot. Missing bounds or unknown cost basis means incomplete. An exit of a held stock (stop, invalidation or stale thesis) is never left waiting on paperwork: when the answer is incomplete, ask again with the missing bounds filled in from the preview and the headroom, and if it is incomplete twice escalate by re-asking with every bound written out explicitly, journal it and notify the owner; only an approve releases the order.
6. Recheck current native status, allocation, the session and the thesis after review. Changed intent/allocation, disconnected/disabled/uncertain state, a closed session or expired approval means no order and new review. Otherwise take one fresh preview for the exact approved request. Verify side/stock/amount match, amount <= approved maximum, the estimated round-trip cost inside the approved cost bound and the approval deadline is unexpired. A preview inside the bound places the order; only one above it, or a changed stock, side or amount, blocks it. Missing fields or out-of-bounds means no order; journal and notify, require new review next tick, never chase the market. Do not insert a slow consultation after this fresh preview.
7. Place the order once while within all bounds: `trading212(action="order", side="<buy|sell>", symbol="<TICKER>", amount="<exact decimal>", max_slippage_pct="<approved>")`. A buy's amount is the EUR to spend; a sell's amount is stock units. The default is a DAY limit order priced through the reference price, which the service cancels if it has not filled after a bounded wait; a zero or partial fill is a normal result, never retried in the same tick. Use `order_type="market"` only if Trading 212 definitely refuses limit orders: keep the same size, journal that finding and notify the owner. Native guards still enforce enabled state, US stocks in the EUR account only, the regular session, ledger allocation, the 40% asset cap below 1000 equity (or half-equity cash reserve, maximum three positions and one-sixth-equity new-position limit from 1000) and the 2 total-equity floor, not cumulative losses. Never bypass a refusal or enlarge an order. At most one new entry per tick, and one order per call; zero trades is valid.
8. Read history. Pending/uncertain is not a fill: reconcile through the native service and stop until resolved; never retry the order. The real fill price and fees come from the Trading 212 order result, not from the reference price: journal market evidence, review verdict/bounds/deadline, previews, amounts, costs, the real fill against the reference price, order IDs and outcome or no-trade reason. Notify failures, stale/invalid approvals, stop thresholds, missing funds and account restrictions, not every empty tick.

Trading style (owner instruction 2026-10-28): active intraday trading. Never hard-code the fee: the cost is the preview's `estimated_round_trip_pct`, whose spread and slippage parts are estimates until real fills show them. Take any candidate whose target is at least 1.5 times that measured cost and at least 0.8%, with gross reward-to-risk of at least 1.5:1 and a horizon that ends before the close. Do not wait for a perfect setup, but never trade below the bar just to trade.

Authoring or discovering this playbook starts nothing and changes no financial activation. Only an explicit owner start uses the existing Condor engine at 5-minute intervals; restart_on_boot preserves its existing resume behavior. Generic scheduler defaults never reset or replace the native ledger/run context. Bounded-intent approval is an advisory agent protocol, not server authorization; existing native guards remain authoritative.
